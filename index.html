What this version can do

SHADOW Anime Studio is the first front-end workspace. It is designed to work on mobile and desktop browsers.

  • Choose and preview a local video file.
  • Write and edit timed subtitle lines; export SRT.
  • Save story concepts, scenes and character notes in this browser.
  • Preview text-to-speech using voices available on the device.

What needs a future engine

Automatic speech recognition, translation, speaker separation, AI dubbing, voice consistency, lip-sync, animation generation and final video rendering need separate models/services and significant processing. A static website alone cannot provide unlimited AI generation for free.
